///How Agentim works

One human interface. A whole company behind it.

Every team runs itself. Each team has an AI team-leader. The team-leaders talk to Mike — your AI chief of staff — over Slack, Telegram, or WhatsApp. Mike coordinates every team and is the only interface you talk to. You give direction to one calm operator; he runs the org.

  • You talk to one operator, not a dashboard of bots.
  • Team-leaders report up; Mike resolves and delegates down.
  • Secure by default — every action scoped, logged, revocable.
