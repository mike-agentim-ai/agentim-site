Frontier models quietly override the data you give them — or argue with it. We built a small, cheap model that answers from your context, faithfully, at a fraction of the cost.

The problem

You give the model your data. It ignores it.

When your context contradicts what a model learned in pre-training, most models trust their own memory — not your document. For enterprise RAG, that's the difference between an answer you can trust and one you can't.

In our tests, a frontier model didn't just get it wrong — it refused the data and lectured us, treating an internal document as a "prompt injection attempt." As the document got longer, faithfulness got worse.
